# Prior work and the question of priority

The number-field question comes from Daniel R. Johnston's problem 8, posed during Ethan Lee's talk in 2024. Hathi and Lee studied the bias of the raw number-field Mertens product. The future log-product average and the question about a uniform polynomial starting point are subsequent questions. [Problem list, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166)

An earlier construction changes sparse packets in the character of conductor 5, adds a lattice of critical-line zeros and keeps its average positive. The same paper shows that twisting by 5 detects the discrepancy between that model and a genuine large field. Both the single-lattice mechanism and the estimate for a packet are used here. The uniform positive lower bound for the character of conductor 13 is also an earlier result. [Single-packet construction and twist test](https://github.com/leecrossley/split-inert-prime-test-obstruction/blob/v1.0.0/PROOF.md) · [Two base-field estimates](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-positive-averages-5-13/blob/v1.0.0/PROOF.md)

There is extensive literature on prescribing zeros of Euler products with completely multiplicative coefficients. Bochkov treats finite-valued coefficients, including real signs for restricted divisors in an open strip; Andersson develops a general Mittag-Leffler construction for Helson zeta functions. Prescribing meromorphic divisors alone predates this note. The constraints here include two related real sign tables on the ordinary primes, a specified boundary line for their two grids, four joint counts within the smoothed quadratic error and a positive future average throughout the claimed interval. [Bochkov](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12847) · [Andersson](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15713)

The added step allocates one packet between the two $\chi_5$ classes so its untwisted sign mass and its twisted sign mass can be chosen independently at every index. That supplies both zero patterns and both finite prime-side tests. It also permits a favourable rise of order $\log D$ while the original average stays positive. Searches on 21 November 2026 covered prescribed zeros of Helson zeta functions with real signs, simultaneous Dirichlet twists, prime-race models and central zeros of quadratic Mertens averages. No earlier result combining these properties was located in the sources checked. The construction is a limit on these tests, and priority remains open to other references.
