# Prior work and the scope of the result

Daniel R. Johnston's Problem 8 from the 2024 Comparative Prime Number Theory Symposium asks where the prime-ideal Mertens product first falls below its main term. It includes the question of a bound over a family. The present note studies the additional information that a decreasing average starting at 2 detects a negative integral, and the time needed for that integral to turn negative. [Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4)

Hathi and Lee study oscillation and bias in a fixed number field. Büthe treats the ordinary prime reciprocal sum. Zhao studies integrated Mertens errors and the signs of their means. Dahl and Kala's character moments and Granville and Soundararajan's residue approximation supply the arithmetic distribution inputs used here. [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Büthe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01277) · [Zhao](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40993-025-00640-y) · [Dahl and Kala](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02424) · [Granville and Soundararajan](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206031)

The earlier optimal-starts note gave the equivalence with a finite negative interval and the upper bound $S\ll(\log d/\log\log d)^2$ when the start is 2. The sharp-deadline note characterised $A_\ast$ and fixed a particular power weight, including its precise exponent-dependent deadline. The new claims concern the sharp lag after the first dip, the stronger worst-case upper bound given any successful decreasing weight at 2, and the growing lower construction with quantitative control of the discriminants. [Optimal starts](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-optimal-starts/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Sharp deadlines](https://github.com/leecrossley/mertens-power-average-deadlines/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

The example uses the real quadratic class-number formula and the split Mellin integral for the even quadratic theta series. The latter expression, its truncation error $3e^{-25\pi}$ at $\lfloor5\sqrt d\rfloor$ and the fundamental unit of this family are derived in §4 of the optimal-starts proof. The primitive is concave between norm jumps. Its future after 1021 is immaterial to identifying the first negative endpoint. [Formulae and error estimates](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-optimal-starts/blob/v1.0.0/PROOF.md)

The priority check on 21 November 2026 covered the primary works above, the comparative prime number theory bibliography, and searches for first Mertens crossings, cumulative logarithmic errors, earliest negative integrals, Yokoi fields and lag bounds in the discriminant. I found no earlier matching statement. Absolute priority and independent review remain open. [Annotated bibliography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08729)
