# Comparison with earlier results

Daniel R. Johnston posed Problem 8 during Ethan Lee’s talk at CPNTS in June 2024. He asked about the first sign change of the raw Mertens product in these two fields and about bounding families of first changes. Hathi and Lee studied raw Mertens-product oscillations and logarithmic bias in number fields and computed bias data for these same examples. The previously released raw crossing calculation locates the dip in each. [Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Raw crossing calculation](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/ef3e53c/README.md)

Tianyu Zhao proves positivity results for integrals from $2$ to $X$ against $dx$ and discusses number-field extensions. Here the lower endpoint moves and the integration runs to infinity against $y\thinspace dt/t^2$. The earlier signed-prime work derived the kernel and bounded the inverse-square zero mass for discriminant 5. An earlier interval calculation covered a finite initial interval in a different field, $d=2380853$, where the average does eventually turn negative. [Zhao, §§1 and 8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18903) · [Earlier mass bound](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/ef3e53c/results/signed-prime-tails/GRID.md) · [Finite initial interval](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/tree/ef3e53c/results/fixed-weight-delay-2380853)

The claims specific to this note are strict positivity for every real start in both named fields, the quantitative margins, and the unique global minimiser for discriminant 5. A November 21, 2026 search of the cited papers, number-field Mertens mean-value and bias work, and the maintained first-crossing collection found no earlier result giving these conclusions for this weight. The analytic argument is a written proof with reproducible interval certificates. The novelty search is bounded and independent peer review remains open.
