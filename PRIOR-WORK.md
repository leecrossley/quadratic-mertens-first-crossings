# Prior work

Daniel R. Johnston's Problem 8 asks for first sign changes of a Mertens product in number fields. Hathi and Lee study the oscillation and bias of that product. This paper studies how much a model with assigned prime signs can tell us about a future weighted average. [2024 problem list](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.03530v1) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166)

Helson zeta functions give ordinary-prime Euler products with completely multiplicative coefficients. Bochkov obtains prescribed zeros and poles with a finite set of coefficient values, including real signs for conjugation-symmetric data in the region of his theorem. Andersson realises prescribed divisors for a single Helson zeta function with unimodular coefficients. Meromorphic continuation, prescribing zeros, and the use of signed prime assignments have clear antecedents. Ford and Konyagin study hypothetical zero configurations that obstruct conclusions in prime races. [Bochkov](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12847) · [Andersson](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15713) · [Ford and Konyagin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08981)

The first split/inert construction used a single lattice of zeros and the second controlled a twist by 5. The second paper records explicitly that testing against 13 detects its base. The uniform positive margin in field 13 is taken from the corrected base result. The conductor term for each genuine Dirichlet base is the classical zero count. [Original construction](https://github.com/leecrossley/split-inert-prime-test-obstruction/blob/v1.0.2/PROOF.md) · [Previous two-character model](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-prime-disagreement-model/blob/be7bb70d5e9030f2b7f5869112da25c27efbf09d/PROOF.md) · [Positive base average](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-positive-averages-5-13/blob/f7233ab4e1c85b9b37bffdaa11c4559d7713bd62/PROOF.md) · [Bennett, Martin, O’Bryant and Rechnitzer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.02989)

The additional construction matches two moments on every square class for a growing set of reference characters. This replaces the nearly principal twist by 13, preserves a strict positive average at all starts and gives nonprincipal base prime sums to every fixed secondary test. The sparse flips impose separate zero grids in one prime assignment, with the period adjustment dictated by the conductor-17 replacement. Both the bounded correlations through a prescribed horizon and the estimate when the grids start immediately are given in the proof.
