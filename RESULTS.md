## Further results

- [A prime average that stays positive past 222 trillion](results/fixed-weight-delay-2380853/)
- [A signed-prime test for a negative Mertens average](results/signed-prime-tails/)
- [How the nearest zero and central multiplicity affect the first negative start](results/signed-prime-tails/NEAREST-ZERO.md)
- Under RH and quadratic Dirichlet GRH, the averages in discriminants 5 and 13 are positive for every real start from 2. [Corrected proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-positive-averages-5-13/blob/f7233ab4e1c85b9b37bffdaa11c4559d7713bd62/PROOF.md) [Original release](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-positive-averages-5-13/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
- A central zero or a sufficiently close pair constrains the scalar prime test in large quadratic fields. A separate prime-sign model gives obstructions to tests at several frequencies. [Hypotheses and proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/split-inert-prime-test-obstruction/blob/v1.0.2/README.md) [Release](https://github.com/leecrossley/split-inert-prime-test-obstruction/releases/tag/v1.0.2)
- Under RH and quadratic Dirichlet GRH, the weighted disagreement with discriminant 5 rises and falls before a fixed power of each sufficiently large positive fundamental discriminant. A late average in a Yokoi field requires a finite adverse lead of order at least the square root of the log discriminant. [Proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-prime-disagreement-windows/blob/2914547d5e178d52394b3c61e427361c070166f2/PROOF.md) [Archived release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24416496)
- An ordinary-prime model permits two independently chosen zero patterns and a large rise in the weighted lead. Its average stays positive from 2 onwards with a double central zero. For each fixed positive exponent, a sufficiently close pair gives positivity from 2 up to that power of the reference parameter. The construction assumes the critical-line property for the four fixed factors of conductors 1, 5, 13 and 65. [Proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-prime-disagreement-model/blob/84bedc24243643b2c09ecd9318b9276dd825f427/PROOF.md) [Archived release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24416498)
