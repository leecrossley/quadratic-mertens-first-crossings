# Literature and scope

Daniel R. Johnston posed Problem 8 during Ethan Lee's talk at the 2024 Comparative Prime Number Theory Symposium. The problem asks for first negative prime-ideal Mertens errors, including a family bound. The decreasing-weight starting point is a further question. [Hamieh, Kadiri, Martin and Ng, Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4)

Hathi and Lee study oscillation and limiting distributions for a fixed number field. Diamond and Pintz prove oscillation for the rational product. Zhao studies integrated errors in Mertens' theorems and extensions involving quadratic characters. These works provide background on individual errors and their signs. [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Diamond and Pintz](https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.687) · [Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18903)

Dahl and Kala's family model and character moments, Granville and Soundararajan's short Euler product and the earlier resonance method are inputs. Earlier notes gave density one with an arbitrarily slowly growing upper bound, fixed first-crossing windows of positive lower density, and fields staying positive to a growing superlogarithmic scale. Those statements leave the end of a growing positive interval, the earliest weighted start, and their ratio undetermined. [Dahl and Kala, Proposition 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.02424#page=11) · [Granville and Soundararajan, Proposition 2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206031#page=7) · [Soundararajan](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3990) · [Earlier fixed windows](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/yokoi-mertens-crossing-bounds/PROOF.md) · [Earlier growing lower bound](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/yokoi-mertens-long-positive-intervals/PROOF.md)

The contribution claimed here is a two-sided growing first-crossing window together with a prescribed earliest-start ratio, a single explicit successful weight and an unweighted lower count in each discriminant shell. The local probability model, its transfer estimate, the ordinary prime number theorem and the critical-line inputs are credited in the proof.

The priority check on 21 November 2026 covered the named primary papers, their public citation neighbourhood, the comparative prime number theory bibliography, and searches combining prescribed sign profiles, Mertens product, first crossing, weighted integral, prime ideals, real quadratic fields, Yokoi and resonance. I found no prior statement giving this prescribed ratio or count. Unindexed and unpublished results remain possible.
