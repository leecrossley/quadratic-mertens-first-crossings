# Delayed Yokoi averages with small quadratic twist correlations

Under RH and quadratic Dirichlet GRH, which Alpöge's announced Dirichlet GRH theorem supplies, many squarefree Yokoi fields have a positive Mertens average at every real start from 2 through a range longer than every fixed power of the log discriminant. At sufficiently large starts in that range the untwisted bias exceeds all the weighted correlations with nonprincipal primitive quadratic characters of conductor up to a fixed power of the start. The fixed power of the conductor test may be chosen in advance. The proved lower bound for the field count is a vanishing fraction of the Yokoi discriminants in the interval. The constants are not computed. The question at a fixed power of the discriminant remains open. [Maintained proof](PROOF.md) [Prior work](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-delay-small-character-correlations/blob/c7d40ff8b5a22a65e1d5da4fbe17144161f06507/PRIOR-WORK.md)

The stand-alone release contains the proof and two calculations of the complete weighted tails for the discriminant 2,380,853. In the unpacked release run `sh verify-release.sh` with the pinned dependency installed. Three supporting lemmas have separate Lean checks.
