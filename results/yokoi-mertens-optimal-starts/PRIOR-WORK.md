# Literature and scope

Johnston's 2024 Problem 8 asks when a prime-ideal Mertens product first falls below its main term. The earliest start for a weighted integral of its log error is a distinct quantity. Hathi and Lee treat oscillations and bias for fixed fields. Zhao treats integrals of Mertens errors, including quadratic-character analogues. Diamond and Pintz study oscillations of the rational prime product. [Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18903) · [Diamond and Pintz](https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.687)

The integrated explicit formula and the prime ideal theorem of Grenié and Molteni supply the spectral and convergence inputs. Representing monotone weights by initial intervals, integrating explicitly between successive jumps, continued-fraction reduction, the class-number formula and the theta identity are established tools. The new use of the one-sided loss bound is to certify a minimum over an unbounded interval from finitely many norm checks and to remove the logarithmic factor from the starting-point implication. [Integrated explicit formula](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.4465#page=2) · [Prime ideal theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.4463#page=3)

Earlier repositories gave the ordinary first crossing for $d=9413$ and a conductor-dependent bound for every real quadratic field. This release computes the optimal *weighted start*, certifies the finite extremizer, and supplies its full reproducible census. An earlier local calculation for $d=9413$ had already excluded starts through 98. That weaker observation is not claimed as a new result here. [Earlier finite-window calculation](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-crossing-bounds/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Earlier all-field bound](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-conductor-bound/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
