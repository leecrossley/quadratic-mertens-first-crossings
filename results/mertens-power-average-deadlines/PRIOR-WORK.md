# Literature and scope

Daniel R. Johnston posed Problem 8 during Ethan Lee's talk at the 2024 Comparative Prime Number Theory Symposium. It asks for the first negative error of the prime-ideal Mertens product and a bound across a family of fields. The question treated here fixes a starting point and assumes a particular average is already negative. [Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4)

Diamond and Pintz studied oscillation in the ordinary Mertens product. Büthe gave an explicit first sign change bound in the rational case. Hathi and Lee studied the fixed-field product, conditions for oscillation and its limiting distribution. Zhao studied integrals of the Mertens errors. These papers concern closely related errors or smoothing operations. [Diamond and Pintz](https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.687) · [Büthe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01277) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18903)

The family moment comparison and approximation to the residue are due to Dahl and Kala and to Granville and Soundararajan. The construction of a positive infinite local pattern uses the effective ordinary prime number theorem. The additional discriminant-dependent estimate in §2 uses the integrated zero formula from the earlier optimal-starts note. Only that estimate is conditional, on the critical-line property for the Riemann zeta function and $L(s,\chi_d)$ (field GRH). [Dahl and Kala, Proposition 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.02424#page=11) · [Granville and Soundararajan, Proposition 2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206031#page=7) · [Johnston and Yang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01980) · [One-sided integrated zero estimate](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-optimal-starts/blob/v1.0.0/PROOF.md)

The contribution claimed here is the sharp family deadline at a prescribed negative power average, its finite-pattern formula and its small-exponent asymptotic. The comparison bound in arbitrary fields exploits the gaps between possible ideal norms. The earlier optimal-starts result allowed arbitrary decreasing weights and retained the field discriminant in its deadline. Earlier prescribed crossings let the starting point vary with the discriminant. [Optimal starts](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-optimal-starts/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Prescribed crossings](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-prescribed-crossings/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
