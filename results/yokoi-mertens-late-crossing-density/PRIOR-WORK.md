# Prior work and the mathematical contribution

Daniel R. Johnston posed the family problem during Ethan Lee's talk at the 2024 Comparative Prime Number Theory Symposium. Hathi and Lee study oscillation and bias of the prime-ideal Mertens product for fixed number fields. Zhao studies integrals of errors in Mertens' theorems. Here the random object is a varying quadratic field and the event involves its entire positive prefix and a subsequent negative integral. [Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Zhao](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40993-025-00640-y)

Dahl and Kala establish the independent-prime model and the family moment comparison. Granville and Soundararajan treat the distribution and extreme values of quadratic $L$-values at one and give the short Euler product. These supply the unconditional arithmetic input for the law. The far end of the integrated field error uses the critical-line property for the Riemann zeta function and $L(s,\chi_d)$, which [Alpöge's announced Dirichlet GRH theorem](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf) supplies. Every theorem here rests on that input. [Dahl and Kala](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02424) · [Granville and Soundararajan](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206031) · [Grenié and Molteni](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4463) · [Independent formal checks](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/)

The earlier sharp-deadline note constructs the constant $A_\ast$. The lookahead note proves a deterministic lower bound on the first negative integral endpoint and a sign prescription attaining the necessary negative area. This note proves a natural density for the joint event, the second term of its doubly logarithmic decay, the growing-shell version, and the concentration of the conditional error curve on one deterministic function. The high-moment estimate, non-atomic limit and matching recovery cost are given in the proof. [Sharp deadlines](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24412091) · [Lookahead](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24412165)
