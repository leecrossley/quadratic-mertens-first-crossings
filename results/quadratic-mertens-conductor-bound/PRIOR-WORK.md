# Literature and scope

Daniel R. Johnston posed Problem 8 during Ethan Lee's talk at the 2024 Comparative Prime Number Theory Symposium. Alia Hamieh, Habiba Kadiri, Greg Martin and Nathan Ng recorded it in their problem list. It asks when the prime-ideal Mertens product first drops below its main term in two fields and asks for an upper bound on the first sign change in a family of fields. The quantity compared here is the same product with the same threshold. [Problem 8, pp. 4–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4)

**Oscillation and earlier bounds.** Harold Diamond and János Pintz proved oscillation for the ordinary rational prime product. Jan Büthe gave a numerical interval containing a sign change for the rational sum of prime reciprocals. Shehzad Hathi and Ethan Lee established large positive and negative scaled errors for a fixed number field under a zero-location hypothesis, using the classical Littlewood–Ingham argument. Their product comparison also uses partial summation. The earlier explicit cutoff for every real quadratic field was $2^{2^{d^{30000}}}$. [Diamond and Pintz](https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.687) · [Büthe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01277) · [Hathi and Lee, §§2.4 and 4.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.02166#page=7) · [Earlier explicit bound](https://github.com/leecrossley/yokoi-mertens-explicit-upper-bound/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

**Existing estimates.** Grenié and Molteni's integrated formula supplies the zero and gamma terms for the prime ideal counting function. Révész gives effective oscillation results for prime ideal counts. Garcia and Lee give explicit number-field Mertens estimates. Chirre, Valås Hagen and Simonič bound the logarithmic derivative of a Dirichlet $L$-function at 1 on GRH. Levent Alpöge's formal Dirichlet zero-location result, as checked in Andrew Sutherland's audit, supplies the critical-line input for both quadratic factors. The local axiom reports cover the four subsidiary Lean lemmas in this note. [Grenié and Molteni](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4465) · [Révész, Part I, p. 59](https://users.renyi.hu/~revesz/irregprimeidi.pdf#page=3) · [Garcia and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.10313) · [Chirre, Valås Hagen and Simonič](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00819) · [Upstream kernel checks](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/)

**Contribution.** The bounded primitive of the zero sum gives a signed comparison with the product error. In the corresponding seed estimate the discriminant terms cancel, leaving the logarithmic derivative at 1. The negative interval has width of order $(\log d)^{-2}$. Finite-order compact smoothing, a zero count at the selected height and classical simultaneous approximation then give a formula with $\log\log U(d)\ll(\log d)^3(\log\log d)^2$. The individual logarithmic-derivative estimate, explicit formula and approximation theorem are earlier results.
