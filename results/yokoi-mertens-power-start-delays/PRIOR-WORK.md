# Literature and scope

Johnston's original question concerns the first negative value of the prime-ideal Mertens error. The extra question here concerns all power averages with exponents in a fixed compact subinterval of $(0,\infty)$. Diamond and Pintz treated the ordinary product and used Cramér's estimate. Hathi and Lee studied fixed-field oscillations. Zhao treated mean values of Mertens errors. [Johnston's Problem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4) · [Diamond and Pintz](https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.687) · [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18903)

The local splitting model and its family-moment estimate are due to Dahl and Kala. The short Euler-product approximation is due to Granville and Soundararajan. Earlier family constructions produced raw positive intervals and prescribed the ratio between an arbitrary decreasing-weight start and a first crossing at a scale exponential in $(\log D)^{1/5}$. The present delay for the power weights reaches a different scale, exponential in $\sqrt{\log D/\log\log D}$, and every exponent in the prescribed interval switches by the same later start. [Dahl and Kala, Proposition 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.02424#page=11) · [Granville and Soundararajan, Proposition 2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206031#page=7) · [Earlier raw delays](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/yokoi-mertens-long-positive-intervals/PROOF.md) · [Earlier prescribed ratios](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/yokoi-mertens-prescribed-crossings/PROOF.md)

A separate geometric-start result bounds from above the proportion missed by a fixed scan. Here the fields are constructed and counted from below, and the sign is controlled for a continuum of starts and exponents. The new steps are the weaker long tilt suited to the averaged error, the different energy and truncation costs of the two biases, and the growing even moments of prefixes ordered by prime rank. [Geometric scan](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/yokoi-mertens-geometric-starts/PROOF.md)

The 21 November 2026 priority search considered the primary references, the comparative prime number theory bibliography and related citation records, including searches combining power-weighted Mertens means, long positive intervals, short Euler products, resonance, Yokoi discriminants and first passage. I found no earlier statement with this simultaneous delay and change of sign. Public indexing leaves open the possibility of earlier work elsewhere.
