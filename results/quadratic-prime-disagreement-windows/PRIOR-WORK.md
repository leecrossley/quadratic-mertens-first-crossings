# Prior work and the question of priority

Daniel R. Johnston posed problem 8 during Ethan Lee's talk at the 2024 Comparative Prime Number Theory Symposium. The published questions concern the first negative value of the raw Mertens product and its variation in families. The future log-product average and its comparison with the field of discriminant 5 are subsequent questions. [Hamieh, Kadiri, Martin and Ng, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.03530#page=4)

Hathi and Lee study oscillations and logarithmic bias for the number-field Mertens product and give numerical information for 5 and 13. Arango-Piñeros, Keliher and Keyes prove a Mertens product theorem on Chebotarev sets. Zhao analyses integrated Mertens errors over the rationals and in arithmetic progressions, including reciprocal-prime errors for quadratic characters. These establish important asymptotic and sign information for other weights or ranges of integration. [Hathi and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02166) · [Arango-Piñeros, Keliher and Keyes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14747) · [Zhao, §§1 and 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18903)

Devin develops prime races for general analytic $`L`$-functions. Fiorilli and Jouve, especially Theorem 2.1, bound the variance of limiting distributions of Chebotarev errors. Those variances average to infinite time for a given extension. Here the lower estimate holds on a finite interval of logarithmic length $`B\log(3d)`$ in each individual large conductor and concerns an eightfold change in a future reciprocal tail. The diagonal contribution persists at arbitrarily small zero spacings. [Devin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06394) · [Fiorilli and Jouve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.05428) · [Fiorilli on highly biased races](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.6946)

The earlier project gives the prime-power kernel, the fixed-band count of low ordinates, a signed-prime criterion, a local hypothesis for polynomial starts and tests based on a near-central zero. A subsequent construction separates scalar and matrix prime tests from the full field problem; its last section obtains smoothed counts in both disagreement directions by twisting with $`\chi_5`$. The reference field's normalised Mertens average was bounded below in a separate paper. [Signed-prime tails](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/ef3e53c/results/signed-prime-tails/PROOF.md) · [Near-central tests](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/split-inert-prime-test-obstruction/PROOF.md) · [Field 5](https://github.com/leecrossley/quadratic-mertens-first-crossings/blob/main/results/yokoi-mertens-positive-averages-5-13/PROOF.md)

The result claimed here is the finite-horizon two-sided change, the lower bound for its mean square and the resulting necessary adverse lead on a finite prime window. The explicit formula, phase rotation, Fejér kernel, diagonal inequality and logarithmic zero count are standard tools or earlier ingredients. Searches on 21 November 2026 covered prime-race oscillation in terms of conductor, finite-time Chebotarev variance, Mertens errors for quadratic characters and Yokoi first crossings. No earlier statement of these bounds was located in the sources checked. Priority remains open to earlier references.
